Baoding tracking viewer

This offline component reuses the saved single-, two- and three-source trajectory table used for the supplementary tracking movie. It does not recompute localization or alter the underlying measurements.

Use the tabs to switch scenario, drag the canvas to rotate, scroll to zoom, and move the sample slider to inspect a time point. The APCE uncertainty ring is a scalar marginal width shown at the current APCE position.

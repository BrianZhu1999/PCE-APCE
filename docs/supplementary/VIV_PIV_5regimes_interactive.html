Data and processing details

Public VIV–PIV dataset: 10.57745/HPA87O. All five views use the paper’s saved APCE seed 0 results and actual measurement locations. A 40 × 20 candidate lattice retains 751 valid spatial points, corresponding to 1,502 scalar velocity observations. The reference field has 201 × 416 spatial points.

Colour limits remain fixed through time and identical across all three panels. Streamwise and transverse bounds match the paper’s Figure 5 or corresponding Supplementary figures. Speed bounds use the same snapshots, as in Supplementary Movie 6. Values outside the displayed interval saturate at the endpoints.

The viewer shows every tenth field frame and the final frame; field display uses every fourth spatial index plus the domain endpoints. Probe spectra use all 1,000 original samples. The fields are continuous reconstructions; separate observation-interruption forecasts are not relabelled onto these traces. Playback is illustrative and does not change physical timestamps.

Strouhal number is calculated from the transverse wake-probe spectral peak as St = fpeakD/U∞. The cylinder diameter is 0.05 m. U∞ is the mean valid upstream streamwise velocity for x/D < −0.8 over the full record. Spectral peaks and Strouhal values are checked against the saved paper source table.
